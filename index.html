  • T. Marsault, L. Schoeffel,
    « Complex Lies, Real Physics: The Role of Algebra Complexification »,
    SciPost Physics Lecture Notes (2026). • [PDF Article]
    In relativistic quantum field theory, the classification of elementary particles rests on the representations of the Lorentz and Poincaré groups, yet the formal passage from real spacetime symmetries to complexified Lie algebras is frequently presented as an opaque technical device. This work reconstructs algebra complexification from first principles, clarifying the exact interplay between real forms, chirality, and spinor sectors (Weyl, Dirac, and Majorana fields). By uniting algebraic rigor with physical transparency, it establishes how spacetime geometry itself rigidly constrains the spin and field architecture of elementary matter.
    En théorie quantique des champs relativiste, la classification des particules élémentaires repose sur les représentations des groupes de Lorentz et de Poincaré, mais le recours à la complexification des algèbres de Lie demeure souvent perçu comme un simple artifice technique. Ce travail reconstruit la démarche depuis ses fondements, en éclairant avec précision le rôle des formes réelles, de la chiralité et des différents secteurs spinoriels (champs de Weyl, Dirac et Majorana). En articulant rigueur algébrique et lecture physique, il montre comment la géométrie même de l'espace-temps dicte le spectre de spin et la structure des champs de matière.
  • L. Schoeffel,
    « From the photon mirage to the proton mass: A pedagogical approach to the trace anomaly and confinement »,
    SciPost Physics Lecture Notes (2026). • [PDF Article]
    While the Higgs mechanism explains the masses of elementary fermions, over 95% of the visible mass of the universe resides in nucleons and arises dynamically from the strong interaction of nearly massless quarks and massless gluons. Focusing on the energy-momentum tensor and its quantum trace anomaly, this article provides a self-contained, pedagogical contrast between quantum electrodynamics and quantum chromodynamics. It demonstrates why the trace anomaly vanishes on asymptotic QED photons—leaving light strictly massless—whereas non-perturbative QCD confinement and gluon field dynamics condense this quantum breaking of scale invariance into the ~1 GeV mass gap of the proton.
    Si le mécanisme de Higgs rend compte de la masse des fermions élémentaires, plus de 95 % de la masse visible de l'univers provient des nucléons et émerge dynamiquement de l'interaction forte entre quarks quasi sans masse et gluons de masse nulle. À travers une analyse pédagogique et autonome du tenseur énergie-impulsion et de son anomalie de trace quantique, cet article met en regard l'électrodynamique et la chromodynamique quantiques. Il montre pourquoi l'anomalie de trace s'annule sur les photons asymptotiques de QED — préservant leur masse nulle — tandis que le confinement non perturbatif de QCD et l'énergie des champs gluoniques condensent cette brisure quantique d'invariance d'échelle en la masse de ~1 GeV du proton.
  • M. Ait Tamlihat, G. Ez-Zobayr, L. Schoeffel, Y. Tayalati,
    « Searches for extra-dimensional excitations in light-by-light scattering »,
    Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) (2026), arXiv:2601.03110.
    Warped extra-dimensional theories such as the 5D Randall-Sundrum model offer a geometric resolution to the electroweak hierarchy problem and predict a dynamical scalar excitation of the compactified inter-brane distance—the Radion—whose phenomenological couplings had long suffered from conflicting normalizations and gauge choices across the literature. This paper delivers a complete, self-consistent theoretical reformulation of the 5D Radion, proving the equivalence between the modulus and brane-bending gauges while rigorously combining the conformal trace-anomaly coupling to photons with Higgs-curvature kinetic mixing. Translating this unified formalism into LHC ultra-peripheral heavy-ion collisions, our work establishes the first direct LHC exclusion contours on the Radion via elastic light-by-light scattering.
    Les théories à dimensions supplémentaires courbées, telles que le modèle 5D de Randall-Sundrum, proposent une solution géométrique au problème de la hiérarchie électrofaible et prédisent une excitation scalaire dynamique de la distance inter-branes — le Radion — dont les couplages phénoménologiques souffraient de conventions de jauge et de normalisations divergentes dans la littérature. Cet article établit une refondation théorique complète et cohérente du Radion 5D, démontrant l'équivalence entre les jauges de module et de déformation de brane tout en intégrant rigoureusement le couplage induit par l'anomalie de trace et le mélange cinétique Higgs-courbure. Appliqué aux collisions ultra-périphériques d'ions lourds au LHC, ce travail aboutit aux premières limites d'exclusion directes du LHC sur le Radion par diffusion lumière-par-lumière.
  • M. Ait Tamlihat, G. Ez-Zobayr, L. Schoeffel, Y. Tayalati,
    « Relativistic Vorticity in the Quark-Gluon Plasma: Symmetries, Explosive Dilution, and Azimuthal Spin Alignment », arXiv:2607.03716 (2026).
    « Relativistic Hydrodynamics and Vorticity Dynamics in High-Energy Heavy-Ion Collisions: A Collective Flow Perspective », arXiv:2607.04273 (2026).
    Non-central ultra-relativistic heavy-ion collisions create the most vortical fluid ever produced in nature—a quark-gluon plasma (QGP) endowed with immense orbital angular momentum (ω ~ 1021 s−1)—whose spin-vorticity coupling imprints measurable global and local spin polarization onto emitted hyperons, yet linking microscopic spin alignment to the rapid collective expansion of the plasma remains a subtle theoretical problem. Across these two companion studies, we develop a 3D relativistic viscous hydrodynamic framework that tracks vorticity generation symmetries, explosive longitudinal and transverse dilution, and azimuthal spin alignment throughout the QGP evolution. Our analysis clarifies how collective flow anisotropies sculpt local vorticity patterns and provides quantitative diagnostics connecting hyperon spin observables to the fundamental transport coefficients of the deconfined medium.
    Les collisions non centrales d'ions lourds ultra-relativistes engendrent le fluide le plus tourbillonnaire jamais observé dans la nature — un plasma de quarks et de gluons (QGP) doté d'une vorticité extrême (ω ~ 1021 s−1) — qui imprime aux hypérons émis une polarisation de spin mesurable, mais dont le lien avec l'expansion collective rapide du milieu reste délicat à modéliser. À travers ces deux études complémentaires, nous développons un cadre hydrodynamique relativiste visqueux 3D décrivant les symétries de génération de la vorticité, sa dilution explosive et l'alignement azimutal de spin au cours de l'évolution du plasma. Ce travail éclaire la façon dont les anisotropies de flot collectif façonnent la vorticité locale et fournit des observables quantitatives reliant la polarisation de spin aux coefficients de transport fondamentaux du milieu déconfiné.

    Predicted in 1936 by Euler and Heisenberg as a quintessential non-linear quantum signature of the QED vacuum—wherein photons scatter elastically via virtual charged-particle loops despite carrying no electric charge—high-energy light-by-light scattering (γγ → γγ) had eluded direct experimental observation for over eight decades. Initiating and co-leading this program within ATLAS, I leveraged the coherent Z4-enhanced electromagnetic fields of ultra-peripheral Pb-Pb collisions at the LHC to isolate ultra-clean exclusive diphoton final states. This effort progressed from the first experimental evidence (Nature Physics, 2017) to the definitive direct observation (PRL, 2019) and precision differential cross-section measurements (JHEP, 2021), establishing a landmark test of non-linear QED and opening a highly sensitive new window onto axion-like particles and physics beyond the Standard Model.
    Prédite dès 1936 par Euler et Heisenberg comme l'une des manifestations non linéaires les plus emblématiques du vide quantique — des photons diffusant élastiquement par l'intermédiaire de boucles virtuelles bien qu'électriquement neutres — la diffusion lumière-par-lumière à haute énergie (γγ → γγ) échappait à toute observation directe depuis plus de quatre-vingts ans. En initiant et co-dirigeant ce programme au sein d'ATLAS, j'ai exploité les champs électromagnétiques cohérents (amplifiés en Z4) des collisions ultra-périphériques Pb-Pb au LHC afin d'isoler des états finals diphotons exclusifs d'une grande pureté. Cette démarche a conduit de la première mise en évidence (Nature Physics, 2017) à l'observation directe définitive (PRL, 2019), puis aux mesures différentielles de précision (JHEP, 2021), établissant un test de référence de la QED non linéaire et ouvrant une voie compétitive pour la recherche de particules de type axion et de nouvelle physique.

  • Finite-Size Effects on the Equivalent-Photon Spectrum at the LHCEffets de taille finie sur le spectre de photons équivalents au LHC,

En formulant un traitement complet en paramètre d'impact intégrant les facteurs de forme électromagnétiques du proton et la probabilité de survie non inélastique sur tout le spectre de masse invariante, cette étude a atteint un excellent accord quantitatif avec les mesures de dileptons exclusifs au LHC et s'est imposée comme une référence phénoménologique pour les processus photon-photon.

Articles de recherche fondamentale & Essais monographiques

Standalone theoretical inquiries written in English, exploring how fundamental symmetries, mathematical structure, and the quantum vacuum govern the emergence of macroscopic order across fluid dynamics and condensed matter:

Scale Symmetry, Vorticity & the Regularization of 3D Fluid Flows

From Navier-Stokes Singularities and Turbulent Cascades to the Limits of Continuum Idealization
• Research Paper in English: Scale Symmetry in 3D Flows (PDF)

At first glance, fluid mechanics appears to belong to the settled canon of classical physics; yet the question of whether a smooth three-dimensional incompressible flow governed by the Navier-Stokes equations can develop a finite-time singularity—the core of the Clay Millennium Problem—exposes a profound tension between mathematical idealization and physical reality. Analyzing three-dimensional vortex stretching, scale invariance, and the turbulent cascade across spatial scales, this paper probes the precise frontier where the continuum description confronts its own limits. Rather than viewing potential blow-up as a mere analytical pathology, it develops a physical regularization perspective showing how symmetry constraints and dissipative cutoffs restore dynamical coherence—echoing a deeper epistemological truth of theoretical physics: singularities are rarely endings of nature, but rather luminous signposts pointing toward hidden symmetries or a more fundamental layer of reality.

Research & Teaching Philosophy (Personal perspective)

Vision de la recherche & de l'enseignement (Perspective personnelle)

My scientific and pedagogical path has been shaped by an enduring commitment to the foundations of physics, where mathematical rigor engages in continuous dialogue with phenomenological reality. Trained at the École Normale Supérieure (Ulm), I carried out my first major milestones in research at the heart of quantum chromodynamics, nucleon tomography, and international collider collaborations — coordinating the diffractive physics and QCD working groups at DESY (H1) and later at CERN (ATLAS). These experimental efforts culminated notably in the first direct observation of light-by-light scattering at the LHC, a threshold where the most subtle aspects of quantum electrodynamics meet the search for new physics.

Over the years, this approach has broadened organically toward foundational theoretical inquiries. Whether exploring spacetime geometry through extra dimensions, relativistic vorticity in the quark-gluon plasma, the physical regularization of singularities in 3D fluid dynamics, or spontaneous symmetry breaking and macroscopic quantum coherence in condensed matter (QED of liquid water), my research follows a singular unifying thread: understanding how gauge symmetries, hydrodynamics, and geometry govern the organization of matter, from subatomic scales to emergent phenomena.

This perspective on physics is inseparable from my vocation as an educator and mentor. From teaching engineering students at ENSTA Paris or EPFL to delivering advanced lectures at Mohammed V University in Rabat and serving as an oral examiner for the École Polytechnique entrance examination, I regard teaching as a demanding art of intellectual emancipation. Conveying the algebraic power of groups and representations, or examining with students why “nature loves to hide,” stems from the same commitment: guiding young minds toward conceptual clarity, cultivating an appetite for depth, and passing on, with generosity and rigor, a sense of the beauty of physical laws.

On a more personal note, this scientific journey reflects a deeper inner quest: the pursuit of truth that does not stop at mathematical formalism alone, but remains attentive to what transcends measure. Whether experienced in contemplation, through lifelong dialogue with philosophy, through close study of foundational texts, or in listening to human vulnerability, this path seeks to unite analytical precision with inner reflection. In the face of multiple opportunities offered to me across diverse institutions, whether in senior academic or executive leadership roles, I have consistently chosen the complete freedom of fundamental research, the long horizon of contemplation, and direct intellectual mentorship. To understand the physical world and to accompany others ultimately arise from the same posture: a humble and exacting fidelity to what elevates thought, sharpens our awareness of reality, and gives genuine purpose to the journey.
